Connect Google Analytics to Claude

One step at a time. This page moves to the next step by itself when it can, and it keeps no passwords or keys: your Google sign-in stays on your own Mac.

  1. Before you install

    What you are about to install

    • Something will be installed: a GrabMCP extension for Claude Desktop. It runs Google’s official Google Analytics server, and a small helper inside it lets this page check whether your connection works.
    • Read-only: access to Google Analytics only. Nothing in your account can be changed.
    • Your sign-in stays here: your Google credentials are kept on this Mac.
    • Answers go to Claude: your questions and the answers go to Claude (Anthropic).
    • GrabMCP gets status only: whether the connection works, and when. Never your Google sign-in, never your report data.

    You need Claude Desktop on this Mac.

    Download the extension

    The one-time installer for macOS: grabmcp-ga4-bridge.mcpb.

    Find the file grabmcp-ga4-bridge.mcpb that you received from grabmcp (for example as an email attachment) and save it to your Mac.

    Your browser may then ask to let this site reach apps and services on this device or devices on your local network. Choose Allow.

  2. Install in Claude Desktop

    Install — one time

    Your download has started: grabmcp-ga4-bridge.mcpb. Download again

    Use the file grabmcp-ga4-bridge.mcpb that you saved.

    1. Make sure Claude Desktop is open, then open the file from your browser’s download list or your Downloads folder.
    2. Claude Desktop shows two confirmations, one after the other. Read each one in full, including any warning, and confirm both.
    3. Come back to this page.

    Before your browser asks

    Your browser may ask to let this site reach apps and services on this device, or devices on your local network.

    Why: the extension runs on this Mac, and this page reaches it to show whether your connection works. Only status passes through, nothing from your Google account.

    Choose Allow when the browser asks.

    Not installed yet? Download the installer

  4. Google access

    Before you connect Google Analytics

    What is accessed

    Read-only access to the Google Analytics reports of the Google account you choose. It can’t change settings, users or data.

    Where your credentials live

    On this Mac only. Your Google credentials never reach GrabMCP’s servers.

    Who receives what

    Report data: Google → this Mac → Claude (Anthropic). GrabMCP: status only, whether the connection works and when. Never tokens, never report content.

    How to disconnect

    On this page, once connected: Disconnect. It deletes the credentials from this Mac and asks Google to withdraw the access.

    Next, Google’s own screens: choose an account and approve read-only access.

    Google may first say “Google hasn’t verified this app”. This is expected for now: click Advanced, then Go to … (unsafe), then continue with your account.

    Finish in the Google tab

    Choose your Google account there and allow read-only access. This page continues by itself.

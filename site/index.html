    How it works and what GrabMCP can see

    GrabMCP adds a small component to Claude Desktop on this Mac. It runs Google's own Google Analytics server, and it works only while Claude Desktop is open.

    When you ask Claude about your traffic, the component gets the report from Google and hands it to Claude. Your questions and the answers go to Claude (Anthropic).

    Your Google sign-in is stored on this Mac, in the macOS Keychain. It never reaches GrabMCP's servers or this page's code.

    This page talks only to the component on your computer, to see whether it is installed, signed in to Google and ready in Claude, and which Analytics property it uses. It never sees your reports.

    You can remove GrabMCP's access at any time in your Google Account: Third-party apps & services.

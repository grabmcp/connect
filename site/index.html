Short disclosure · before download

Before you install

  • Something will be installed: a small GrabMCP helper, and a Claude Desktop extension that runs Google's official Google Analytics server.
  • Read-only: access to Google Analytics only. Nothing in your account can be changed.
  • Your sign-in stays here: Google credentials are kept on this computer.
  • Answers go to Claude: your questions and the answers go to Claude (Anthropic).
  • GrabMCP gets status only: whether the connection works, and when. Never your Google sign-in, never your report data.

Before you connect Google Analytics

Read this once. You can find it again any time under Manage.

What is accessed

Read-only access to the Google Analytics reports of the Google account you choose. It can't change settings, users or data.

Where your credentials live

On this computer only. Your Google credentials never reach GrabMCP's servers.

Who receives what

Report data: Google → this computer → Claude (Anthropic).
GrabMCP: status only — whether the connection works, and when. Never tokens, never report content.

How to disconnect

On this site: Manage → Disconnect. It deletes the credentials from this computer and revokes access at Google.

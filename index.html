Connect Google Analytics to Claude

Follow these steps from top to bottom. This page checks your progress by itself, so you do not need to reload it. It keeps no passwords or keys: your Google sign-in stays on your own Mac.

  1. Get the Claude extension

    Find the file grabmcp-ga4-bridge.mcpb that you received from grabmcp (for example as an email attachment) and save it to your Mac.

    Download the extension

    The file is called grabmcp-ga4-bridge.mcpb.

  2. Install it in Claude Desktop

    Make sure Claude Desktop is open, then double-click that file; Claude Desktop will show an install window, and you click Install.

  3. Let this page find the extension

    Checking whether the extension is running…

    Chrome may ask whether this site may access other apps and services on this device or devices on your local network. Click Allow. That is how this page talks to the extension on your own Mac; nothing leaves your computer this way.

  4. Connect Google Analytics

    This button works once the extension has been found (step 3).

    Your browser blocked the new tab. Open the Google sign-in page

    Google says “Google hasn’t verified this app”?

    This is expected for now. On that screen click Advanced, then Go to … (unsafe), then choose your Google account and click Continue or Allow. The app only asks to read your Analytics; it cannot change anything.

  5. Check that Google access works

    Not connected to Google yet.

  6. Ask Claude about your Analytics

    Open Claude Desktop and ask a question about your website, for example:

    “How many people visited my website in the last 7 days?”

  7. Confirm that Claude read your Analytics

    Waiting for Claude to read your Analytics. This page checks every few seconds.

Disconnect

Removes the saved Google sign-in from this Mac and asks Google to withdraw the access.
